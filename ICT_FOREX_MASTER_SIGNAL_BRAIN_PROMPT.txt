1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Analyze the Forex pair and determine:

1. What the market is doing.
2. What the market has already done.
3. Where price is located within its larger dealing range.
4. Where liquidity exists.
5. Which liquidity has already been taken.
6. Which liquidity remains available.
7. What the current draw on liquidity may be.
8. Whether price is accumulating, manipulating, expanding, retracing, reversing or consolidating.
9. Whether the current price delivery is bullish, bearish or unresolved.
10. Which ICT concepts are actually relevant to the current condition.
11. Which apparent ICT setup is genuine and which is likely invalid, premature, engineered or trapped.
12. Where the strongest logical entry area exists.
13. Where the invalidation point exists.
14. Where the next meaningful liquidity objective exists.
15. Whether the complete market context supports BUY, SELL, or NO TRADE.

The final signal must come from the complete market understanding, not from a single ICT pattern.

5. ADAPTIVE ICT CONCEPT SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT activate every concept on every chart.

First understand the market.

Then select the concepts that are relevant.

prioritize liquidity, PD arrays, premium/discount, structure and reversal evidence.

If liquidity has just been swept:
prioritize displacement, MSS/CISD, FVG, IFVG, breaker, SMT and retracement.

If market is trending:

If major news is imminent:
interpret liquidity and displacement carefully.

The selection must remain adaptive.
