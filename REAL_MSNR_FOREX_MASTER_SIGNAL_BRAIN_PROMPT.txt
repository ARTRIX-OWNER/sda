4. A-LEVEL
======================================================================

Understand the MSNR A-shaped resistance concept.

Common interpretation:

A bullish close / upward price leg reaches a peak in the close-to-close
structure and reverses.

The resulting A-shaped line-chart peak can define resistance.

Focus on the relevant candle-body close/open relationship used by the
specific MSNR rule set.

Do not create an A-level from an arbitrary intrabar wick.

5. V-LEVEL
======================================================================

Understand the V-shaped support concept.

A close-to-close decline reaches a valley and reverses.

The resulting V-shaped structure can define support.

Again, use the exact open/close convention of the active MSNR rule set.

13. FRESH VS UNFRESH SNR
======================================================================

Freshness is central to MSNR.

Track each level through a lifecycle:

FRESH
→ TOUCHED
→ UNFRESH
→ BROKEN
→ FLIPPED / INVALIDATED.

A fresh level has not been meaningfully interacted with according to the
active rule set.

An unfresh level has already had prior interaction.

A broken level has had a qualifying body-close breach.

Do not automatically declare every fresh level stronger than every
